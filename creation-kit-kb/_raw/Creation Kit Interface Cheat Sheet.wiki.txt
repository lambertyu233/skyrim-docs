|style="background-color:lightgray;" colspan=3 align="center"|'''Menu Shortcuts'''
|-
|Save
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+S'''
|Save the active plugin.
|-
|Undo
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+Z'''
|Undo the last action in the current window or field.
|-
|Redo
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+Y'''
|Redo the last Undone action.
|-
|Cut
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+X'''
|Cut the current selection.
|-
|Copy
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+C'''
|Copy the current selection.
|-
|Paste
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+V'''
|Paste the copied item.
|-
|Paste in Place
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+Shift+V'''
|Paste the copied item with no offset.
|-
|Move
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+M'''
|In the Render Window, move the copied item instead of creating a new reference.
|-
|Move in Place
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+Shift+M'''
|In the Render Window, move the copied item instead of creating a new reference. Maintains the same coordinates.
|-
|Duplicate
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+D'''
|In the Render Window, duplicate the selected item.
|-
|Find
|align="center"|'''Ctrl+F'''
|Open the Find/Replace Window.
|-
|Find Next
|align="center"|'''F3'''
|Find the next item in the current search.
|-
|Find Prev
|align="center"|'''F2'''
|Find the previous item in the current search.
|-
|style="background-color:lightgray;" colspan=3 align="center"|'''Camera Controls'''
|-
|Pan 
|align="center"|'''MMB+Mouse<br>Space'''
|Hold the middle mouse button and use the mouse to pan the camera (or hold the spacebar)
|-
|Orbit
|align="center"|'''Shift+Mouse'''
|Hold shift and use the mouse to orbit around a selection
|-
|Zoom
|align="center"|'''Scroll Wheel''' or '''V+Mouse'''
|The Scroll wheel will zoom in/out from the selection
|-
|Focus Camera
|align="center"|'''Shift+F'''
|Snap Camera position to selection bounds
|-
|Top View
|align="center"|'''T'''
|Force a top-down view
|-
|Cycle Views
|align="center"|'''C'''
|Cycle through pre-set camera angles
|-
|Orthographic Projection
|align="center"|'''0'''<br>(zero)
|Toggle between Orthographic/Perspective projection ''(advanced users only)''
|-
|style="background-color:lightgray;" colspan="3" align="center"|'''Object Manipulation'''
|-
|Movement
|align="center"|'''Left-Click+Drag'''
|Click and hold to drag to move the selection on the horizontal XY axis
|-
|Rotation
|align="center"|'''Right-Click+Drag'''
|Click and hold RMB to rotate the selection
|-
|Axis Constraint
|align="center"|'''Z/X/C'''
|Hold Z, X, or C  to constrain movement or rotation to the Z, X, or Y axis.
|-
|Grid Snap
|align="center"|'''Q'''
|Toggle snap-to-grid for movement and rotation
|-
|Scale
|align="center"|'''S'''
|Hold S and left-click drag to scale the selection. Uniform scaling only.
|-
|Drop to Ground
|align="center"|'''F'''
|Drops the selected object to the nearest surface.
|-
|De-Select
|align="center"|'''D'''
|Removes all objects from current selection
|-
|style="background-color:lightgray;" colspan="3" align="center"|'''Gizmo Helpers'''
|-
|Move Gizmo
|align="center"|'''E'''
|Enables gizmo w/Handles for XYZ axes and bi-axis planes
|-
|Rotate Gizmo
|align="center"|'''W'''
|Enables gizmo w/Handles for XYZ rotation
|-
|Scale Gizmo
|align="center"|'''2'''
|Enables scaling Gizmo w/XYZ handles for non-uniform scaling of primitives
|-

|-

|-

|-
|Toggle Collision Geometry

|-
|Toggle Toggle Water

|-

|-
|Toggle Grass
