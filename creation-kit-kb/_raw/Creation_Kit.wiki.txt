The Creation Kit is the editor used to create content for [https://elderscrolls.bethesda.net/en/skyrim The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim]. It's free to anyone with a PC copy of Skyrim and a Steam account.

The current version of the Creation Kit is 1.9.32.0.

==Installing==
The Creation Kit can be found in your Steam Library, in the '''Tools''' section, or by clicking [steam://run/202480 this link].
